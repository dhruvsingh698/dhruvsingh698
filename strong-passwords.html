Creating Strong Passwords and Using a Password Manager

Dhruv Digital Guide · 4 min read

A strong password should be unique to each account. Reusing one password means that a breach on one service can put other accounts at risk.

Make passwords unique

Do not reuse the same password across email, social media, shopping and financial accounts. A breach on one site should not unlock another.

Prefer long passphrases

A longer, memorable phrase made from unrelated words can be easier to remember and harder to guess than a short, predictable password.

Consider a reputable password manager

A password manager can generate and store unique passwords. Choose a trusted provider, protect its main account and understand its recovery process.

Turn on two-step verification

Where available, add a second verification step to important accounts. Keep backup codes somewhere safe and private.

Never share one-time codes

Support staff should not need you to tell them a one-time password or verification code. Treat unexpected requests as suspicious.
