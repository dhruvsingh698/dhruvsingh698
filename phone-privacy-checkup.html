A Simple Privacy Checkup for Your Android Phone

Dhruv Digital Guide · 4 min read

A privacy checkup is a chance to understand what information apps can access. Menu names vary by Android version and phone maker, so use Settings search if a label looks different.

Review app permissions

In Settings, search for “Permission manager” or “App permissions”. Review access to location, camera, microphone, contacts and photos. Keep access only where it makes sense for the app's function.

Use a secure screen lock

Set a screen lock that is difficult for others to guess. Keep your device software updated and use the manufacturer's security features where available.

Protect lock-screen notifications

If private message previews appear on your lock screen, review notification privacy settings so sensitive content is not visible to passers-by.

Secure your main account

Use a unique password and two-step verification for your primary email or device account. Keep recovery options current.

Check before granting access

If an app requests a permission that does not fit its purpose, pause and investigate. You can often deny optional permissions and still use basic app features.

Remember: Settings and features vary by phone model, Android version and region. If a menu is different on your device, search Settings for the relevant term or consult the phone maker's official support page.
