How to Check an App Before Installing It

Dhruv Digital Guide · 4 min read

A familiar-looking icon or a high download count does not guarantee an app is trustworthy. Pause to check the developer, permissions, reviews and the source of the download.

Use trusted app sources

Prefer the official app store or the app developer's official website. Avoid random download pages and unsolicited links.

Check the developer name

Look for the developer's other apps, official website and support details. Watch for names that imitate well-known brands.

Read recent reviews carefully

Look for repeated reports about unexpected charges, intrusive ads, crashes or misleading behaviour. Reviews can be manipulated, so treat them as one signal rather than proof.

Think about requested permissions

A simple wallpaper app may not need contacts or microphone access. Consider whether each permission is necessary.

Keep the phone protected

Install system and app updates, and use built-in app scanning features where available. If you suspect an app is unsafe, uninstall it and review account activity.

Remember: Settings and features vary by phone model, Android version and region. If a menu is different on your device, search Settings for the relevant term or consult the phone maker's official support page.
