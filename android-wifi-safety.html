Safer Habits When Connecting to Public Wi-Fi

Dhruv Digital Guide · 4 min read

Public Wi-Fi can be convenient in stations, cafés and other shared places. Treat unfamiliar networks carefully and avoid assuming that a network is private just because it has a familiar name.

Confirm the network name

Ask the venue for the correct Wi-Fi name. Attackers can create lookalike network names, so do not connect based on a familiar name alone.

Prefer secure websites and apps

Use websites with HTTPS and keep apps updated. HTTPS protects information in transit to the site, but it does not make a fraudulent site trustworthy.

Avoid sensitive activity on unfamiliar networks

When possible, wait until you are on a trusted connection before handling especially sensitive accounts. Follow your bank's and service provider's security guidance.

Turn off automatic joining

Review saved networks and disable automatic connection to unfamiliar public networks when you do not need it.

Forget networks you no longer use

Remove old public networks from your saved list if you no longer need them.

Remember: Settings and features vary by phone model, Android version and region. If a menu is different on your device, search Settings for the relevant term or consult the phone maker's official support page.
