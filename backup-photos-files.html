How to Back Up Important Photos and Files

Dhruv Digital Guide · 4 min read

A backup is a separate copy of information you would not want to lose. It can be stored in a trusted cloud service, on a computer or on an external drive.

Choose what matters

Make a list of important photos, school files, contacts and documents. Do not assume every app backs up all of its data.

Pick a trusted backup location

Options may include a reputable cloud account, a computer or an external drive. Check storage limits, privacy settings and recovery options.

Check that the backup completed

Look for a recent backup date and open a few files from the backup location. A backup is useful only if it can be restored.

Keep account recovery safe

Protect the account used for cloud backups with a unique password and two-step verification. Store recovery codes privately.

Use more than one copy for irreplaceable files

For especially important files, consider keeping another copy in a separate location. Avoid leaving the only backup beside the device it protects.

Remember: Settings and features vary by phone model, Android version and region. If a menu is different on your device, search Settings for the relevant term or consult the phone maker's official support page.
