About this website

Dhruv Digital Guide is an independent educational website created to make everyday technology easier to understand. We publish beginner-friendly articles about Android phones, digital privacy, online safety and practical productivity.

Our editorial approach

  • Use clear language and explain technical terms.
  • Encourage safe, lawful and responsible technology use.
  • Point readers toward official support resources when device-specific instructions are needed.
  • Correct content when we discover an error.

Independence

This website is not affiliated with Google, Android, phone manufacturers, mobile operators or any government agency. Brand names are used only to identify products or services discussed.
